Wenbo Zhu

I am a Computer Engineering PhD student at the University of Southern California. My current work focuses on agile chip development, accelerator systems, and tools that make specialized hardware easier to build, measure, and program.

Going forward, I am especially interested in the compute systems behind physical intelligence: learned models that perceive, predict, plan, and act in the real world under latency and energy constraints. I see embodied AI not only as an algorithmic problem, but also as a systems problem, where model design, inference substrate, efficiency, and deployment constraints must be considered together.

Research & Publications

My work sits at the boundary of hardware architecture, design automation, and workload-driven accelerator design. I am especially interested in practical chip-building flows, memory-centric compute, and accelerators for emerging applications.

PowerFlow-DNN: Compiler-Directed Fine-Grained Power Orchestration for End-to-End Edge AI Inference

Paul Chen, Jeongeun Kim, Wenbo Zhu, Yuanhan Li, Shunyao Huang, Chenjie Weng, Christopher Torng

ISLPED 2026 · paper

Abstract

We developed compiler-directed fine-grained power orchestration for edge AI inference, formulating deadline-constrained DNN execution as an end-to-end inter-layer scheduling problem that coordinates DVFS and power gating under practical accelerator constraints.

Characterizing and Optimizing Realistic Workloads on a Commercial Compute-in-SRAM Device

Niansong Zhang, Wenbo Zhu, Courtney Golden, Dan Ilan, Hongzheng Chen, Christopher Batten, Zhiru Zhang

MICRO 2025 · paper · pdf

Abstract

We characterized and optimized realistic workloads on GEMINI, a commercial-scale processing-in-SRAM associative processing unit, using profiling and architectural analysis to understand its performance and energy-efficiency potential.

Fusion-3D: Integrated Acceleration for Instant 3D Reconstruction and Real-Time Rendering

Shihui Li, Yuchi Zhao, Chong Li, Boyuan Guo, Jie Zhang, Wenbo Zhu, and collaborators

MICRO 2024 · Best Paper · paper · record

Abstract

We built integrated acceleration for neural 3D reconstruction and real-time rendering, continuing a line of work on making NeRF-style workloads practical for real-time and on-device settings.

Instant-3D: Instant Neural Radiance Field Training Towards On-Device AR/VR 3D Reconstruction

Shihui Li, Chong Li, Wenbo Zhu, Bo Yu, Yuchi Zhao, and collaborators

ISCA 2023 · paper

Abstract

We accelerated on-device neural radiance field training for AR/VR 3D reconstruction, with an emphasis on the hardware implications of emerging neural rendering workloads.

RT-NeRF: Real-Time On-Device Neural Radiance Fields Towards Immersive AR/VR Rendering

Chong Li, Shihui Li, Yuchi Zhao, Wenbo Zhu, Yingyan Lin

ICCAD 2022 · paper · pdf

Abstract

We investigated real-time on-device neural radiance field rendering for immersive AR/VR applications, targeting the system requirements of interactive neural 3D graphics.
